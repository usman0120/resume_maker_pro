Local processing, no tracking

Your resume is created on your device. No analytics, ads, external fonts, or third-party scripts are loaded. The hosting provider still receives normal page requests and may record access logs.

You decide what is saved

Drafts stay in memory unless you enable “Remember on this device.” Saved drafts use this browser's local storage, not a database. Avoid saving on shared devices; clear the draft when you are done.

Honest about the limits

Local drafts are not encrypted. Browser extensions, other device users, and shared downloads can expose information. Back up your work, review exports, and only include details you want to share.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you start.

Simple answers. No fine-print surprises.

Is it really free?

Yes. All templates, colors, and exports are available without a subscription, watermark, or account. This is a personal productivity project by Usman, not a paid resume service.

How do I download a PDF?

Choose PDF / Print in the builder. In your browser's print dialog select “Save as PDF,” use A4 or Letter to match the builder, set margins to Default, disable browser headers and footers, and enable background graphics for the closest match. PDF options vary by browser and device.

Can I edit the resume later?

Export a JSON backup and import it next time to restore content and design. RTF opens in many word processors; HTML is a standalone copy; plain text is useful for application forms. PDF and RTF export do not require external libraries.

Will my resume work with applicant tracking systems?

The templates use a single reading order and real text, not a screenshot. This helps readability, but no builder can guarantee results with every ATS. Follow the employer's requested format and verify the exported text.

Can I use it on my phone or offline?

The editor works on phones, tablets, and desktops. Internet is needed to initially load the site; resume editing and export require no server calls once loaded. This is not an installable offline app. Your browser's print support may vary.

Where can I report a problem or get in touch?

Visit Usman's portfolio contact page. It is an external website with its own privacy practices. Do not send sensitive resume information when reporting a bug.
